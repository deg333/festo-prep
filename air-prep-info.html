Festo Safety Exhaust Valve & air prep information

How the machine air-prep unit works: filter, optional CIP on/off valve (V2S1) and the dual-channel MS6-SV-D soft-start safety dump valve (V1S1 / V1S2, PLd).

Indicator lights

Green — 24 VDC good
24 V is present at the coil connector.
Yellow — coil energised
The PLC output to the coil is on.
Blue — Control Reset lamp
Solid and not blinking when the machine air is enabled.

Try it

The 3D simulation runs this unit with the PLC logic, and the pneumatic schematic shows how the valve position blocks slide.

V2S1 — the CIP unit

The first valve, V2S1, is the CIP unit. It has only one coil. The green light on the connector indicates good 24 VDC; the yellow light indicates a signal to the coil (energised). When you press the Control Reset button you should see the two lights on the coil turn on (green and yellow). CIP functionality is optional on a machine.

V1S1 & V1S2 — the main machine air valves

The second set of valves, V1S1 and V1S2, are the main machine air-pressure valves. They give you dual safety redundancy coils and dual proximity-sensor feedback (FS1 and FS2). The connector lights work the same way: green = good 24 VDC, yellow = coil energised.

When you press Control Reset you should see all six indicator lights on all three coils turn on, and the blue light solid and not blinking.

Soft-start

V1S1 and V1S2 are the soft-start safety valves. The set screw controls how fast the soft start is: turning it counter-clockwise, or (+), gives faster flow, so pressure accumulates faster — or instantaneously.

More about the soft start

There is a flow-control screw in the end cap. It generates a gradual pressure build-up of the output pressure p2. The flow, and so the pressure rise, can be adjusted by turning the screw. When p2 reaches about 50 % of the operating pressure p1, the maximum flow rate is enabled.

Switching characteristics & diagnostics

The normal position, “bled off”, is sensed by the proximity switches S1 (FS1) and S2 (FS2): they read 1 (24 V) in the normal position and change to 0 shortly after the pilot valves V1 and V2 are energised. No third sensor (S3) is fitted on this unit.

Electrical connections

The main pneumatic safety solenoid (PLd) block (631) has the two proximity switches and the two coils; the optional CIP coil is block (632).

Safety category

The MS6-SV-D can achieve various categories and safety levels to EN ISO 13849-1, depending on how the directional control valves are monitored — with appropriate integration into the control chain and appropriate linking of the signals for initial-position sensing with the signals for activation (plausibility checking).

Performance Level d / category 3 to EN ISO 13849-1 and EN ISO 13849-2 can be achieved when using sensors S1 and S2.
